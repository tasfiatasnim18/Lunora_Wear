# 1. Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for security, compliance, risk management, and continuous improvement across the Lunora Wear platform.

Its objective is to ensure that security controls remain effective, measurable, auditable, and aligned with business objectives throughout the platform lifecycle.

---

# 2. Objectives

The governance framework shall:

* Define security ownership.
* Maintain policy compliance.
* Manage security risks.
* Support regulatory readiness.
* Enable repeatable security reviews.
* Drive continuous improvement.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

The platform follows these principles:

* Security is everyone's responsibility.
* Governance is evidence-based.
* Policies are version controlled.
* Risk is explicitly managed.
* Compliance is continuously verified.
* Improvement never stops.

Governance should evolve alongside the platform.

---

# 4. Governance Lifecycle

```text id="0m2f4r"
Define Policies
        │
Implement Controls
        │
Measure Compliance
        │
Review Findings
        │
Improve Controls
        │
Update Policies
```

Governance activities should operate as a continuous cycle rather than a one-time exercise.

---

# 5. Security Policies

The governance framework shall maintain policies covering:

* Identity and access management
* Authentication
* Authorization
* Data protection
* Encryption
* Secrets management
* Logging and monitoring
* Incident response
* Secure development
* Infrastructure security
* Backup and recovery
* Business continuity

Policies should have designated owners and review schedules.

---

# 6. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Leadership

Responsible for:

* Security strategy.
* Business risk acceptance.
* Resource allocation.
* Major policy approvals.

## Security Engineering

Responsible for:

* Security standards.
* Risk assessments.
* Security reviews.
* Incident coordination.
* Security metrics.

## Platform Engineering

Responsible for:

* Infrastructure compliance.
* Operational controls.
* Deployment governance.
* Runtime security.

## Application Teams

Responsible for:

* Secure implementation.
* Vulnerability remediation.
* Code quality.
* Application-level controls.

Governance responsibilities should be clearly documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# 7. Risk Management

Security risks should be:

* Identified.
* Assessed.
* Prioritized.
* Assigned.
* Tracked.
* Reviewed.
* Closed or formally accepted.

Risk acceptance should require documented business justification.

---

# 8. Compliance Reviews

Regular reviews should evaluate:

* Policy adherence.
* Security controls.
* Access permissions.
* Infrastructure configuration.
* Vulnerability remediation.
* Operational readiness.

Review findings should generate tracked improvement actions.

---

# 9. Exception Management

Security exceptions should include:

* Description.
* Business justification.
* Risk assessment.
* Compensating controls.
* Approval authority.
* Expiration date.
* Review schedule.

Permanent exceptions should be avoided.

---

# 10. Metrics

Governance metrics may include:

* Policy compliance rate.
* Critical vulnerabilities.
* Mean time to remediate.
* Security review completion.
* Audit findings.
* Exception count.
* Security training completion.
* Incident trends.

Metrics should support informed decision-making.

---

# 11. Documentation Governance

Security documentation should:

* Be version controlled.
* Have assigned owners.
* Include review dates.
* Record significant revisions.
* Reference related standards.

Outdated documents should be revised or retired.

---

# 12. Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement activities include:

* Lessons learned from incidents.
* Architecture reviews.
* Threat model updates.
* Security testing results.
* Audit findings.
* Emerging technology assessments.

Improvements should be prioritized based on business risk.

---

# 13. Governance Meetings

Recurring governance activities may include:

* Monthly security review.
* Quarterly architecture review.
* Quarterly access review.
* Annual disaster recovery exercise.
* Annual policy review.
* Annual risk assessment.

Meeting outcomes should be documented and tracked.

---

# 14. Acceptance Criteria

This document is complete when:

* Governance responsibilities are defined.
* Risk management is established.
* Compliance review processes are documented.
* Metrics are identified.
* Continuous improvement is integrated into governance.
